# A Bouquet for Mary 💐

A romantic, interactive digital bouquet. Marigolds, roses, cosmos, and asters bud at the bottom of the screen, bloom open, and spiral upward in a 3D helix around a glowing golden name — passing behind and in front of it — over a lush flower bed, drifting petals, floating hearts, and fireflies. The name is the only text on screen.

Move your mouse to stir the flowers and trail sparkles; click or tap to plant a new bloom.

**Send it as digital flowers** — the name is customizable through the URL, so one link works for anyone:

```
index.html?name=Sofia
```

A third scene, `moonlight.html`, is a quiet, romantic alternative built from scratch: a single marigold floating on a moonlit lake, golden pollen rising to light the name in the sky, with its reflection rippling in the water, twinkling stars, drifting petals, and occasional shooting stars. Click the water to send a ripple.

There is also a cinematic version: `movie.html` plays a ~30-second film — the first vine sprouts in close-up, the garden climbs and swirls, one marigold blooms in slow motion, every flower opens in a wave, and the name is revealed — then holds on the living garden with a replay button. The ▶ icon on the main page links to it, and `?name=` works there too.

A fourth scene, `amy.html`, is a ray-traced dawn meadow: eight tulips — rose, coral, and blush — grow from the grass and bloom in an arc around the name as the sun rises, every petal marched as real 3D geometry in a WebGL shader and lit with backlit translucency. `?name=` works there too.

No dependencies or build step — just open `index.html` in a browser.
